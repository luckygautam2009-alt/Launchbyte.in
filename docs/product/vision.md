# Vision

LaunchByte is a student-first opportunity discovery and action platform.

## The Problem

Students rely on scattered, unverified sources — forwarded messages,
random social pages, outdated listing sites — to find hackathons,
internships, events, and courses. Listings are often duplicated, stale, or
irrelevant to the student seeing them, and there's no single trustworthy
place to go from "found it" to "applied."

## The Vision

A single platform where every opportunity a student sees is:

1. **Verified** — checked for a legitimate source, current deadline, and
   accurate details.
2. **Personalized** — relevant to that student's skills, interests, and
   goals, not a generic firehose.
3. **Actionable** — trackable from discovery through application, not just
   a link to click and lose track of.

Core philosophy:

```text
Discover → Verify → Personalize → Track → Act
```

## Who It's For

Students actively looking for hackathons, internships, events, and courses
— particularly those currently relying on fragmented, informal sources to
find them.

## What Success Looks Like

A student can open LaunchByte, see opportunities that are actually relevant
and current, understand what's required, and track their applications in
one place — without needing ten other tabs, group chats, or pages open to
do it.
